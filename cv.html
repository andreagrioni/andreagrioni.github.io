Profile

Clinical development professional working where clinical trials meet data science and AI. Building machine-learning models for biomedical data since 2014; at Novartis since 2020, moving from exploratory biomarker analysis in Phase I–III trials to the global team of a Phase III cardiovascular outcome trial.
